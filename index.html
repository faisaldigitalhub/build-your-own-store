Build Your Own Store

Learn how an online store works by building one yourself — five steps, plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, no install, no account, fully offline.

1

Product Catalog

Products come from a data file, not from hand-written HTML. Filter by category.

2

Shopping Cart

Add to cart, change quantities, see the total. The cart is saved in your browser.

3

Checkout via WhatsApp Order

Fill a form, get a ready-made WhatsApp order message. No backend, no payment.

4

Search and Sort

Live search and sorting by price and name, in plain JavaScript.

5

Admin: Add a Product

Add a product from a form — it shows up in the catalog, cart, and search.

All prices in this project are demo prices in PKR, for learning only — not real shop prices.
